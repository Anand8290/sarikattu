# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 23 November 2026_

SplitLite is an open-source app for splitting expenses within a group. This policy explains what it stores.

## What is stored

When you use SplitLite, the app creates an **anonymous account** for your phone. You don't give an email,
phone number or password.

The app stores on its server only what you and your group members enter:

- group names and currencies
- member names, as typed by the group
- expense titles, amounts, dates, who paid and how they were split
- payments recorded between members

This data can be seen by anyone who has joined the same group with its invite code.

## What is not collected

No location, contacts, advertising ID, analytics or crash tracking. There are no ads. Nothing is sold or
shared with third parties.

## Where it is stored

Data is stored with [Supabase](https://supabase.com) and sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

## Deleting your data

Open **Settings → Delete my data** in the app. This removes you from every group and deletes your anonymous
account. Expenses you added stay visible to the other members as part of their group's history. A group
is deleted completely once every member has left it.

More detail: [Delete your data](delete-data).

## Children

SplitLite is not directed at children under 13.
