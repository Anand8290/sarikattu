# Delete your SplitLite data

## In the app

1. Open SplitLite.
2. Tap **Settings** (top right of the groups screen).
3. Tap **Delete my data**, then **Delete**.

What happens:

- You leave every group, and your anonymous account is deleted right away.
- Expenses you added stay in your groups so the other members' balances stay correct. Your name stays on
  them as the group typed it.
- A group with no members left is deleted completely, including all its expenses.

To leave only one group, open it, tap the **⋮** menu and choose **Leave group**.

## If you no longer have the app

Your account was anonymous, so it can't be looked up by name or email. Open an issue at
<https://github.com/Anand8290/splitlite/issues> with your group's invite code and your name in that group,
and we'll remove you.
