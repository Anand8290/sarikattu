SariKattu keeps track of shared expenses for trips, flatmates, dinners and anything else you pay for together.

• Create a group and share the invite link on WhatsApp or anywhere else
• Everyone adds expenses from their own phone
• Split equally between any members
• See who owes whom, with the fewest payments needed to settle up
• Record payments in cash, UPI or anything else to clear balances

No sign-up: your phone gets an anonymous account, so no email or phone number is needed.
No ads, no paid tier, and no limit on how many expenses you add.

SariKattu is open source (GPL-3.0). The code and the server setup are on GitHub, so you can check exactly what it does or run your own server.
